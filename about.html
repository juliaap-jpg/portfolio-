Julia Perez is making Things

Writing and illustration have always been a major part of my life, and it’s difficult to pinpoint exact inspirations. Sometimes my projects are inspired through reflections of myself, sometimes they’re inspired by other pieces I love, sometimes by things that I hate. Outside of original projects, I have an interest in how people take inspiration and are influenced by their own favorite pieces of media, and how fandom spaces inspire creativity in their own right. Whenever possible, I love to help other people bring their own creative visions to life.
